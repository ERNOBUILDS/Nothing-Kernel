Nothing sweet2 v3 performance governor policy (UNBOOTED-TEST)
==========================================================

Classification: source-justified policy port, not a measured performance fix.
The replacement base has never been booted on the intended phone. V2 remains
the booted fallback; historical v2 vendor captures are not new v3 measurements.

Saved vendor init.qcom.post_boot.sh and sweet_postboot.sh select schedutil or
other available governors after boot. The latter starts at sys.boot_completed=1
and writes CPU minima 576000/652800 kHz. Those files establish possible ROM
policy writers; they do not identify the present writer of every maximum drop.

CONFIG_CPU_FREQ_SWEET2_PERFORMANCE retains performance when a valid userspace
request selects another governor on policies 0/6. Other governors stay compiled
and enumerated. Unknown names still return EINVAL. Successful writes must be
followed by scaling_governor readback: the effective selection is performance,
not necessarily the requested name. A rate-limited message identifies task/PID
for intercepted governor requests; it is not a trace of min/max writers.

The legacy CPU_BOOST driver is restored because the saved vendor post-boot script
writes its cpu_boost/parameters interface. Its input defaults remain 40 ms and
1497600/1843200 kHz, unless userspace supplies its own settings. Beta1's separate
CPU_INPUT_BOOST driver is retained, not disabled or retuned. Both act through
ordinary policy adjustment and leave thermal validation in place; coexistence
and runtime effectiveness are untested, not a reason to pin policy minima.

The Kconfig dependency is adapted to MACH_XIAOMI_SWEET, not v2's ARCH_SM6150:
beta1 builds sweet on ARCH_SDMMAGPIE and ARCH_SM6150 is disabled. This scoping
is not evidence of explicit sweet2 hardware support; that remains unverified.

The read-only parameter /sys/module/cpufreq/parameters/sweet2_performance has a
boot-command-line opt-out: cpufreq.sweet2_performance=0. Booting the previous
kernel also restores its governor behavior. No ramdisk service, KernelSU module,
/data script, persistent property or governor/max polling loop is added by this
port. The Image-only ZIP preserves the installed command line and ramdisk.

Performance requests the highest frequency allowed by the existing policy.
The OSM driver reads its hardware LUT and constructs the valid frequency table;
freq_table.c seeds policy/cpuinfo minimum and maximum from that detected table.
The old 1804800/1843200 kHz raised-initial-minimum port is deliberately excluded.
No hard-coded maximum or min=max pin is introduced. The owner's expected
1804800/2304000 kHz maxima are not verified as reachable OPPs in v3.

All user_policy min/max storage, driver validation, cpufreq notifiers, cooling
ceilings/floors and thermal trips retain beta1 behavior. A thermal ceiling may
temporarily collapse effective min/max; the saved user request is reapplied on
policy updates unless another writer changes it. No runtime persistence claim
is made. This governor override can increase battery consumption and heat;
it does not bypass protection or guarantee the requested physical frequency.

No soft thresholds are raised: the complete controlling vendor rule is not
decoded and no fresh temperature/cooling/writer samples are authorized. Do not
assume cool-device caps are thermal, or assume they are not, without those data.

Remaining runtime checks (owner-run, not executed here):
- Boot/root/manager compatibility on the new base and actual policies/top OPPs.
- Governor/min/max/cur_freq readback at boot and +5 minutes and under light load.
- A short per-cluster load with temperatures, cooling states and frequency traces.
- Legal min/max sysfs write/readback, later readback, and attribution of rewrites.
- Compare selectable cpufreq table and OSM LUT against actual reached clocks;
  separate userspace policy, governor, thermal, PM QoS and firmware/LUT limits.

O2/generic arm64/ThinLTO are retained; no O3/A76 experiment is included.
No overclock, GPU/KGSL tuning beyond the process-reference fix, pstore/observer,
thermal bypass, SELinux/AVB/dm-verity change or F2FS safeguard weakening is added.
